# Jester voice trial: findings and fix plan

For the full voice-to-EBI commander workflow, see [commander readiness review](COMMANDER-READINESS-2026-09-28.md).

Review date: September 28, 2026. This is a diagnosis and plan, not a claim that the fixes are built or that the voice experience is ready. The evidence is the local Jester service journal, its turn metrics, the two latest room transcripts, and the current code. The 112 Node and 6 speech checks passed before the trial, but they do not run a long real GPU or Discord conversation.

## What the trial established

| Priority | Finding | Evidence and likely cause | Checked outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Speech recognition stopped reliably hearing the room. | The journal recorded **78 ONNX memory allocation errors from 14:26:03 to 14:32:27 CDT**. Parakeet has a 3 GiB GPU arena cap; `worker/speech.py` catches each input exception, logs it, and continues without telling the voice loop the utterance was lost. The transcript becomes patchy in this interval. This is an arena limit failure; total GPU exhaustion has not been proved. | Benchmark realistic short and long audio on this GPU, bound recognition memory, and recover automatically after an allocation failure. Run a 20-minute two-speaker soak with no repeated errors or silent lost turns. |
| 1 | Jester spoke during a conversation with another person. | After an owner wake, the attention gate keeps accepting most owner speech for 60 seconds. The room transcript shows Jester answering several ordinary side remarks while another person was talking. `src/attention.mjs` only recognizes a few explicit side-talk forms. | End or suspend Jester's follow-up window when another person speaks or the owner clearly ends the exchange. Keep Drew's chosen 60-second maximum for direct follow-ups; require its name again after side conversation. Verify that an ordinary direct follow-up still works. |
| 1 | Spoken dismissal did not reliably dismiss it. | In the trial, the owner said “Disconnect” and later “Jester, shut up, go away.” `src/presence.mjs` accepts only exact “Jester leave” or “Jester disconnect.” The other forms reached conversation/Luna, and Jester kept speaking. | Recognize unambiguous owner-addressed leave phrases locally, including “Jester, shut up, go away”; accept bare “disconnect” only when the immediately active exchange clearly targets Jester, with no intervening side conversation. Stop audio immediately and leave the channel without sending the command to Luna. Keep owner-only checks. |
| 2 | A rapid reply/cancel sequence reached a brain overlap error. | The journal has one “A brain turn is already running.” `Brain.interrupt()` waits for the interrupt request but the prior turn may still be completing when the next `ask()` starts. The transcript includes a cached Luna-unavailable message during this sequence. | Serialize or await completion of the interrupted turn before the next brain request; make rapid barge-ins and corrections produce one current reply without a false unavailable message. |
| 2 | A restart leaves an old transcript marked “in progress.” | Recent transcript files from previous services retain `Ended: (in progress)`. The app close path stops services without calling `RoomTranscript.finish()`. A restart with the owner present starts another transcript. | Close and publish the current transcript on graceful shutdown; mark an orphaned prior file as interrupted on startup without deleting or merging speech. Verify restart while the owner is in the room. |
| 2 | Natural session questions can fall through to generic Luna chat. | The owner asked for help with Drew's audit, corrected the name to Zoro, and got a generic “what kind of audit?” reply. The deterministic router understands exact status/task forms, but this request was not one of them, so Luna had no checked EBI context. | Add a narrow read-only path for natural “help me with / what is happening in [current tag]” requests: resolve the exact current session, fetch its status, and give Luna only checked facts when an open-ended answer is needed. Never send work based on an ambiguous phrase. Verify the exact destination before any later task. |
| 2 | Session discovery has a scale and phrase mismatch. | Jester asks for at most 100 EBI sessions; the live endpoint returned exactly 100, and EBI hard-caps that endpoint at 100 without a continuation token. This can hide an older session, although the total number was not established. Also, the attention gate accepts “Yo Jester” but the session-intent parser does not strip “Yo,” so a status/action phrase with that greeting can miss the router. | Add bounded pagination or an exact-tag lookup for current sessions, with an explicit overflow signal; use the same wake-prefix parser for conversation and owner intent. Test more than 100 sessions and “Yo Jester, what is Zoro doing?” without guessing a target. |
| 3 | EBI lookups briefly failed after service restarts. | Two session-tag fetch failures and five event-poll fetch failures appear near startup. The local sessions endpoint later returned HTTP 200. This looks transient; no persistent outage was established. | Add a bounded startup retry and show a clear “session list unavailable” response if lookup still fails. Confirm it recovers without restarting Jester. |

One decoder-corruption warning appeared. It needs monitoring during the next trial; one warning alone does not justify a decoder rewrite. The former flood of routine session announcements appears in an older transcript; quiet-by-default was loaded later and should be checked in the next trial rather than counted as a new failure.

## Build order: small checked outcomes

1. **Recognition reliability (about 30–60 minutes plus a 20-minute soak).** Reproduce the arena failure using local recorded or synthetic audio of varied lengths, then choose the smallest effective memory bound and recovery behavior. Add a test for the failure signal and worker restart. Keep live room speech and any paid model out of the automated test.
2. **Silence and dismissal (about 30–45 minutes).** Make guest speech close the owner-only follow-up window while retaining the chosen 60-second maximum, and make the observed leave phrases deterministic. Test owner, guest, side talk, interruption, just-listen, talk-again, and leave paths together. The priority is to avoid unexpected speech or action.
3. **Brain cancellation and transcript restart (about 30–45 minutes).** Exercise rapid interrupts against a fake app-server and a clean restart against a fake transcript channel. Ensure no false Luna-unavailable cue and one closed transcript per ended room visit.
4. **Session context and startup recovery (about 45–75 minutes).** Make the observed Zoro-style question read checked EBI facts, fix the wake-prefix mismatch, make current-session lookup complete beyond 100 entries, retain strict owner and exact-thread checks for actions, and retry transient EBI startup failures. Check read-only status before trying one harmless prompt to a disposable session. The EBI endpoint change belongs in its own checked slice and must be coordinated with active EBI work.
5. **Owner trial (10–15 minutes after the fixes).** In Discord, try wake → follow-up → side conversation → wake again, “just listen” and visible mute, “talk again,” the observed dismissal phrases, one-time session update, and a current tag status question. Compare the destination thread and spoken answer with the room transcript and journal. Record any new issues as separate fixes before calling Jester ready.

Engineering time is roughly **3–5 hours plus the soak and owner trial** for the issues already found. This is an estimate, not a completion guarantee; issues Drew observed but has not listed may add work. Do not schedule an overnight build or add guest controls. Keep EBI and other active sessions untouched except for a coordinated endpoint fix and a deliberately chosen disposable voice-routing check.

## Decisions already made for this plan

- Jester stays quiet about routine session changes. “Jester, update me on my sessions” gives one response; an explicit result watch can report once.
- “Jester just listen” keeps transcribing and displays Discord self-mute; “Jester talk again” returns to dormant conversation. “Leave” disconnects, so no mute icon remains after departure.
- A guest's speech ends Drew's follow-up window. Keep the chosen 60-second maximum after Jester finishes speaking, but do not let ambient speech refresh it; Drew can say “Jester” again after a side conversation or lull.
- Do not claim that offline tests prove conversational feel or accurate speech in a real multi-person room. The final owner trial is required.
