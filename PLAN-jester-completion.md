Check: bash scripts/check.sh

Goal: Jester replaces the old voice service without losing its useful transcript features, lets Drew converse naturally and control the right EBI sessions safely, reports meaningful agent events, and survives long sessions and restarts. This is the **master plan**. Work now proceeds in small checked slices; guest conversation and guest control are parked for later.

## What the review found

- ✅ **Working now:** Jester joins with Drew, hears and answers him, supports spoken interruption and leave/join controls, loads Parakeet/Smart Turn/Kokoro/Luna, and writes per-room transcript files. The old voice service is archived; the main EBI bot still runs. A real Jester transcript was checked against allwork's own parser: 29 of 29 recent speech lines parsed.
- ⏳ **The voice still needs its feel test:** eight completed live replies have a median end-of-speech-to-first-audio time of about **2.8 seconds**, including one 10.2-second stall; the handoff target is about 1.5 seconds or less for ordinary turns. Drew has not yet completed the 10–15 minute natural-conversation check.
- ✅ **Streaming and stall cue integrated locally:** completed Luna sentences now reach Kokoro as they arrive after Drew's accepted turn; the cached cue waits for an accepted stall. The live feel check still measures whether the timing is good enough.
- ✅ **Attention and transcript-only mode integrated locally:** `Jester` wakes it, follow-ups have a 60-second window, and `Jester just listen` stops replies and actions while transcription continues. The live A/B check is still required.
- ⚠️ **Transcript parity is integrated locally:** Jester now edits an Auto Transcripts attachment, shows a recording notice with Pause, and prunes files after 30 days. The live room check is still required. Allwork now uses Goku for its existing idea trigger.
- ✅ **Owner session controls are integrated locally and loaded:** exact current-tag lookup, one-time status on request, faithful prompts with delivery receipts, new-session creation, model changes, stop, close, history lookup, and a single-session follow-on task are implemented with owner-only checks. Routine event updates stay silent. The real voice-room trial still has to verify that each action feels right and reaches the intended thread.
- ⚠️ **Remaining quality gate:** the parser accepts explicit command forms rather than every natural paraphrase. Result watches can cover up to 100 named/currently running sessions per request; Jester quotes only explicit test steps visible in each final reply and links the threads, so a reply with no test steps cannot produce a reliable test list. Drew's real voice trial remains. Guest conversation and grants remain deferred by Drew's choice.
- ✅ **EBI interfaces are integrated locally:** read-only current-session and turn-journal APIs, durable spoken delivery receipts, long prompt validation, first-turn backend choice, and one-thread stop are present in the running bot. An in-flight network failure can still leave an action uncertain; Jester reports that uncertainty and avoids a blind duplicate send.

The Phase 1 plan deliberately stopped at talking in Discord because `HANDOFF.md` requires a feel test before EBI control. Calling that partial build “Jester done” and retiring the old feature before restoring its privacy/channel behavior was my mistake. This plan closes that gap.
